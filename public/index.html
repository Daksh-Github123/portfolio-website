BLADE: Botanical Leaf Analysis & Disease Evaluator

2026

Independent research project

Lead Engineer & Co-researcher

A handheld, fully offline crop-disease scanner. Paper under review.

  • Built on a Raspberry Pi 4B with a 5 MP camera, a touchscreen interface and a custom 3D-printed enclosure. A pretrained MobileNetV2 convolutional neural network classifies a leaf photo into 38 classes (26 diseases, 12 healthy states) across 14 crops.
  • The pipeline runs input checks, combines five augmented views of each image (test-time augmentation) and applies confidence thresholds, so the device answers "uncertain" rather than giving a wrong diagnosis.
  • I rebuilt the device (camera swap, reworked internal layout, full software audit, Git version control, speed optimization) and redesigned the study as a controlled experiment.
  • Research question: why do plant-disease models that score ~99% on lab images fail in real fields? Is the cause the leaf or the background? BLADE photographs the same leaves while changing one condition at a time (background, framing, lighting), which turns a correlation found in earlier studies into a controlled test.

Raspberry Pi 4B · 38 classes · 14 crops · fully offline
